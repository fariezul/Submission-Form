        About This Activity

        Zero Defect Rush is a revision challenge for Chapter 3, Corrective Action and Process Improvement. Thirty questions, ten minutes, one mark each — and a perfect 30 / 30 is the only pass.

        Every question is taken directly from the lecture slides: the 7 QC Tools, the PDCA Cycle, 4W1H problem analysis, CAPA and Lean Manufacturing. A wrong answer is never explained and the correct one is never shown, so the way through is to go back to the slides and study the point you missed. Attempts are unlimited and the questions change each time.
