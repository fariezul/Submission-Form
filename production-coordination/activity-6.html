        About This Activity

        REVISION 1 prepares you for Test 1 in aircraft composite production. Each attempt draws 20 questions from a bank of 23. You have 20 minutes and need 20 / 20 to pass.

        Revise coordination, direct and indirect materials, the seven QC tools, Lean Manufacturing, the eight wastes and the aerocomposite production process. For a workflow question, choose the sequence that fills all three blanks. You can enlarge the diagram on a phone. Attempts are unlimited, and questions change each time.
