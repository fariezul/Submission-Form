Adaptive Digital Module · DQP10013 Unit 4

ADM Interactive Learning

Premix and prepreg activities for the Diploma in Polymer Composite Engineering Technology, TVETMARA Masjid Tanah

Overview

These activities support DQP10013 Fundamentals of Polymer Composites, Unit 4: Premix and Prepreg (U4L8 and U4L9). Students explore the materials, practise the handling routine and make decisions in a workshop story, on a phone or a laptop.

Each activity opens in its own page, works without signing in, and has sound effects. Turn your sound on.

Activities

Hotspot: Know the 3 Premix Materials

Tap the SMC sheets, the DMC/BMC dough and the carbon prepreg roll on a real workshop bench. Each point opens a data card, then a comparison table. U4L8.

Open the hotspot

Playbook: Prepreg Handling

Seven stages from delivery to disposal, with checklists to tick and an out-time calculator for your roll. U4L9.

Open the playbook

Simulation: Friday Afternoon

You are in charge of a roll of carbon/epoxy prepreg. Make five decisions and watch its out-time, moisture risk and your safety. U4L9.
